# dice-game

A Python-based dice betting game where players gamble virtual money by guessing dice rolls.

    In this game, the player starts with a balance of $100 and can:

- Choose how many dice to roll
-Choose how many sides each die has
-Make a single guess for all dice
-Place a wager per die
-Earn or lose money based on results
-Encounter random “lucky events” (bonuses or penalties)

    The game continues until the player:

-Runs out of money, OR
-Chooses to quit

    **Game Rules**
-Start balance: $100
-Each round:
    Choose number of dice
    Choose number of sides (minimum 2)
    Make a guess (1 to number of sides)
    Place a wager per die
-Each die is rolled individually
-If a die matches the guess:
    Player wins money
-If it does not match:
    Player loses money
-Random events may occur after each round:
    Small bonus
    Big bonus
    Jackpot
    Penalty
    Nothing happens
